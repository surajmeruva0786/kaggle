About Dataset
Overview
The Global Superstore Dataset is a comprehensive, multi-dimensional collection of retail transaction records designed for business intelligence, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and predictive modeling. Tracking over 50,000+ orders across 140+ countries, this tabular dataset simulates the operational footprint of a multinational e-commerce retailer. It serves as an ideal training ground for data visualization, supply chain optimization, and market basket analysis.

Dataset Structure & Schema
The data is structured into three primary tables to mimic realistic corporate data architectures:

Orders Table (Transactional Logs)
Temporal Features: Order Date, Ship Date (Essential for time-series forecasting).
Geographic Demographics: Country, State, City, Region, Market.

Product Classification: Category, Sub-Category, Product Name.

Financial Performance: Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit, Shipping Cost.

Logistics Details: Order Priority, Ship Mode.

Returns Table (Reverse Logistics)
Features: Returned (Status flag), Order ID, Market.
Application: Analyzes post-purchase friction, churn metrics, and product quality issues.

People Table (Sales Governance)
Features: Person (Regional Manager), Region.
Application: Useful for categorical filtering, cohort analysis, and regional performance reviews.

Core Data Science Use Cases
Time-Series Forecasting: Predict future Sales and Profit trends using regression or advanced ARIMA/Prophet modeling frameworks.

Customer Segmentation: Build RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) clustering models to classify B2B and B2C segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office).

Supply Chain & Logistics: Calculate delivery latency (Ship Date minus Order Date) to evaluate shipping efficiency across different shipping tiers and geographic regions.

Interactive Dashboarding: An excellent baseline resource for building portfolio projects on Tableau, Power BI, or Streamlit.
