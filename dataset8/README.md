# ₿ Cryptocurrency Market Intelligence (2018–2026)

## Overview
3,800+ monthly records covering 50 major cryptocurrencies across 23 categories from January 2018 to March 2026 — spanning 3 complete market cycles. Includes OHLCV, market cap, on-chain metrics, sentiment scores, Fear & Greed Index, and exchange netflows.

## Files
- `crypto_monthly_prices.csv` — Monthly OHLCV + on-chain + sentiment per coin
- `coin_metadata.csv` — Coin profiles with ATH, drawdown, avg returns
- `global_market_summary.csv` — Monthly global crypto aggregates
