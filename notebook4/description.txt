About Dataset
🌍 Global Renewable Energy Transition Tracker (2000–2025)
50 countries · 26 years · 25 columns · Analysis-ready CSV

This dataset tracks the global shift from fossil fuels to renewable energy across the top 50 electricity-producing countries from 2000 to 2025. It combines data from Our World in Data, Ember, and IEA into a single clean CSV — enriched with CO₂ savings estimates, year-on-year growth rates, and 59 real policy milestones (Paris Agreement, IRA, Energiewende, COP26 pledges, and more).

Why this dataset?
In 2025, solar and wind grew fast enough to meet all new global electricity demand for the first time. China's solar capacity surpassed 1 TW. Wind and solar hit 30% of EU electricity, beating fossil fuels. This dataset lets you study and visualise that transformation in detail.

Key facts you can discover
China's solar electricity grew 450x from 2000 to 2025 (1.2 → 1,175 TWh)
The US Inflation Reduction Act (2022) coincides with the steepest wind growth inflection in US history
Norway has maintained >98% low-carbon electricity since 2000 (hydropower)
Germany's carbon intensity dropped from 585 → <300 gCO₂/kWh post-Energiewende
India's renewables share jumped from 14% (2000) to ~30% (2025), driven almost entirely by solar and wind post-2015
Suggested use cases
Time-series forecasting — predict solar/wind growth by country
Policy impact analysis — did the IRA, EEG, or COP26 pledges actually bend the curve?
Cross-country dashboards — who is leading, who is lagging?
CO₂ savings estimation — how much carbon has the transition avoided?
Clustering — group countries by transition speed, income level, or strategy
EDA practice — clean, beginner-friendly for Pandas, Seaborn, Plotly
