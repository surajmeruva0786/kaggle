# 🏀 NBA Player & Team Statistics (1980–2026)

## Overview
50,785 player-season records and 1,410 team-season records covering 46 years of NBA basketball. Includes per-season stats for 45 historical legends plus 8,000 regular players, team win/loss records with advanced metrics, career profiles, era-by-era summaries, and salary data — making this the most comprehensive synthetic NBA dataset on Kaggle.

## Files
- player_season_stats.csv — Per-season stats for all players (50K rows)
- team_season_records.csv — Team win/loss and advanced metrics per season
- career_profiles.csv — Career summary for All-Star caliber players
- era_summary.csv — How basketball statistics evolved across 7 eras
- mvp_winners.csv — MVP award history

## What Makes This Unique
- 45 named NBA legends with realistic career arcs calibrated to actual statistical profiles
- 7 historical eras modelled (Showtime → Jordan → Warriors → Modern)
- Advanced metrics: PER, Win Shares, BPM, VORP per player-season
- Salary data from 1980–2026 capturing real market inflation
- Team metrics: Net Rating, Pace, Offensive/Defensive Rating, True Shooting%
- 3-point revolution fully captured in team and player data
